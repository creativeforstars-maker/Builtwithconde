SOP 1Daily Outbound Routine60 min · Every morning 8:00–9:00▴
1
Warm-up + safety check (5 min)
Open Instagram and engage naturally first — reply to 5–10 comments, react to stories. Never start the day cold-blasting DMs. Account limits: max 60 new DMs/day, never more than 15–20/hour. Account under 3 months old: start at 20/day, add 10/week.
2
Follow-ups first (15 min)
Lead Tracker → filter prospects on Day 3, 5 and 7. Send the exact follow-up for their stage, update status. Follow-ups convert 2–3× better than cold openers — they always go first.
3
New Message 1s (25 min)
40–60 new prospects. Qualified = UK/US founder or exec · 35–50 · real business visible (team, product, clients) · active in the last 7 days · talks about workload, travel or low energy. Disqualify: athletes, gym regulars, no real company behind the profile. Personalise line 1 with something from their last 2 weeks of content — never copy-paste it.
4
Replies + tracker hygiene (15 min)
Answer every reply with the DM scripts. Move each lead to its true stage, book calls via Calendly inside the conversation. Definition of done: inbox at zero, tracker current, day logged (new sent · follow-ups · replies · calls).
SOP 2Story Sequence Publishing45 min active · Every 7–10 days▾
1
Build the 7 stories (25 min)
Use the Story Sequence templates in Canva. Update Story 5 (Proof) with the latest client result — never run the sequence twice with the same proof. Export as individual images/videos.
2
Publish spaced across the day
Story 1 at 8:30am → one every 90–120 min → Story 7 lands 7–9pm. Never post two at once; completion rate collapses.
3
Work the poll in real time (15 min)
DM every YES within 60 minutes: "Hey [Name], saw your vote — is this something you're actively trying to fix right now?" Also DM anyone who replies to any story — a story reply is hotter than a cold opener. Log them as Replied.
4
Score it next morning
Good sequence = 25+ poll votes and 5+ conversations started. Below that twice in a row → rewrite Stories 1–3 (the hook chain) before running it again.
SOP 3Content Publishing3 hrs/week · Batch on Tuesday▾
Mon
Plan + prep (30 min)
30-Day Calendar → pick the week's 5–6 pieces. Write captions from the templates. List exactly what needs filming.
Tue
Batch film (60 min)
Film both Reels plus 3–4 B-roll clips in one session. Minimum two takes per hook. One session, whole week covered.
Tue/Thu
Edit + publish (30 min each)
Subtitles always · hook on screen in the first 2 seconds · under 45s · CTA with the keyword. Publish 7–9am or 6–8pm UK. Quality bar: would a founder stop scrolling in the first 2 seconds? If not, re-cut the hook.
Daily
Engage (10 min)
Every comment answered within 1 hour of posting. Every content DM answered same day. Comments are lead generation, not admin.
SOP 4Sales Call Preparation15 min · 30 min before every call▾
1
Research the person (5 min)
Their company (what it does, rough size), their content (energy and fitness signals, what they complain about), their pre-call answers. Write down 2 specific details to reference in the first 5 minutes.
2
Prepare the screen share (5 min)
Client results ready — before/after photos + the 9kg case study. The 12-week plan overview open. Close every other tab. Nothing on screen but proof and the plan.
3
Environment + mindset (5 min)
Quiet room, camera at eye level, headset, water. Re-read the 5-phase framework once. Diagnose for 15 minutes before mentioning the programme. Silence after the price.
SOP 5Weekly Review30 min · Every Sunday evening▾
1
Fill Weekly Metrics (10 min)
All numbers, no gaps: outbound (sent, replies, reply %), stories (votes, conversations), content (posts, best piece by DMs), sales (calls, shows, closes), revenue.
2
Decide with thresholds (15 min)
Reply rate < 7% → rewrite Message 1 before Monday. Show rate < 80% → tighten the pre-call sequence. Poll < 25 votes twice → rebuild the hook chain. Best content piece → schedule a variation next week. The numbers decide, not the mood.
3
Reset the board (5 min)
Every pipeline lead in its true stage. Dead leads → Closed Lost with the reason written down. The tracker only works if it's true.
SOP 6Client Wins & Testimonials20 min · The day a client hits a milestone▾
1
Capture it same-day
The number (kg, weeks in, energy score), a direct quote, a screenshot of the message where they told you. Ask permission in the same conversation: "Can I share this? First name only if you prefer."
2
Bank it in two formats
A 2-line version (number + quote) for stories and DMs · a 60-second version (context → what changed → result) scripted for a Reel.
3
Deploy within 7 days
The win goes into Story 5 of the next sequence and one Authority post. Fresh proof outsells old proof — never sit on a win.
